Our Story

A Legacy of Excellence

Founded in 1948, Horizon University has stood at the forefront of higher education in Central Asia for over seven decades. From its earliest days as a modest research institute, it has grown into a comprehensive university that serves more than 18,000 students across a broad range of disciplines.

Our campuses combine historic architecture with modern research facilities, creating an environment that honors tradition while embracing innovation. We are committed to cultivating critical thinkers, creative professionals, and responsible global citizens.

Horizon University's graduates occupy leadership positions in government, business, science, and the arts around the world — a testament to the enduring quality of education delivered here in Tashkent.

Why We Exist

Our Mission & Values

At Horizon University, our mission is to create, preserve, and disseminate knowledge in the service of society. We believe that education is not simply the transfer of information — it is the cultivation of wisdom, character, and the capacity for lifelong learning.

We are committed to an inclusive environment where students of all backgrounds can discover their potential. Our interdisciplinary approach encourages collaboration across departments, cultures, and borders, ensuring our graduates are prepared for the complexities of a connected world.

Through cutting-edge research, community engagement, and global partnerships, Horizon University strives to address the most pressing challenges of our time — from climate change to public health, from digital transformation to social equity.
